Cobalt is more than the web and mobile apps. Your data is yours — pipe it into your favorite tools or build on top of it.

## Connect a third-party app

Connect Cobalt to apps that support OAuth or accept API keys. Authorize once, and the app reads your financial data through Cobalt.

→ [Connect a third-party app](/docs/guides/third-party-apps)

## Connect your chatbot

Plug Cobalt into Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, or any MCP-compatible client. Ask your AI assistant about your spending, positions, and net worth — it queries Cobalt directly.

→ [Connect your chatbot](/docs/guides/chatbot)

## Build your own personal finance app

Use the TypeScript SDK or REST API to build custom dashboards, agents, or automations on top of your financial data.

→ [Build your own personal finance app](/docs/guides/build-your-own)
