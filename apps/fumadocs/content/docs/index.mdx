Cobalt connects your accounts, transactions, brokerage data, and market research in one place. Same data syncs across web and mobile.

## 1. Sign up

Create a free account at [cobaltpf.com](https://cobaltpf.com). No card required.

## 2. Add accounts

Two ways to add accounts. Mix and match.

- **Third-party provider** — connect via Plaid (banks, brokerages, credit cards). Balances and transactions sync automatically.
- **Manual** — add an account by hand. Enter balances and transactions yourself. Good for accounts Plaid doesn't cover (e.g. private investments, foreign banks).

Accounts added on web show up on mobile and vice versa.
